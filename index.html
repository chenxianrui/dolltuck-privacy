Dolltuck 隐私政策

生效日期及最后更新日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

本政策适用于由陈显锐提供的 Dolltuck iOS App、与 Dolltuck 有关的反馈邮件及本政策网页。Dolltuck 无需注册独立账号即可记录收藏。

1. 我们处理哪些信息

  • 收藏和设置。你录入或保存的娃娃、娃衣、搭配、娃柜名称及布置、分类、尺寸、购入价及币种、购入日期、来源和备注，以及头像、图片和相关设置，用于展示、编辑、整理、计算花费和生成预览。语言等偏好保存在设备上。购入价是你手动填写的收藏记录。
  • 照片和相机。你选用相册时，App 通过系统选择器读取你选中的图片。你使用拍照时，App 会请求相机权限。图片用于录入收藏、编辑头像、布置娃柜和导出。裁剪、去背景和手动搭配预览在设备上处理。你选择保存导出图时，App 会请求向相册添加图片的权限。
  • 订阅与版本。购买、续订、付款和恢复购买由 Apple 处理。App 读取 StoreKit 提供的产品、交易和订阅状态，以确认可用权益。开发者不会收到银行卡号或 Apple 账户密码。检查更新时，App 会向 Apple 查询应用标识及对应商店地区的公开版本信息，本机版本在设备上比较。
  • 意见反馈。你确认发送邮件后，开发者会收到发件地址、主题、正文和你自行附加的内容，用于回复、排查问题和改进 App。App 打开邮件草稿，由你决定是否发送。请避免在反馈中附带与问题无关的个人信息。

Dolltuck 不出售个人信息，也不使用广告追踪或第三方行为分析 SDK。

2. 存储、同步和信息接收方

收藏及图片保存在设备上。当 iCloud 同步可用时，相关记录、布置、图片与可同步的设置通过 Apple CloudKit 保存到与你 Apple 账户关联的私有 iCloud 数据库，以在设备间同步。开发者不通过 Dolltuck 的服务后台读取你的私有收藏。设备未能启用该存储时，App 可使用本机存储。你可以在 Apple 的 iCloud 设置中管理同步和存储。Apple 隐私政策

订阅、更新查询和 iCloud 由 Apple 提供。反馈邮件经你选择的发信服务传送，并由接收邮箱服务处理。你主动使用系统分享、把图片另存到相册，或把教程提示词复制到外部工具时，所选服务会按其规则处理你提供的内容。Dolltuck 不会主动把照片发送给教程中提到的生成工具。

本政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会记录访问者的 IP 地址以保障安全。网页本身没有加入广告、分析脚本或表单。Apple、邮箱服务及 GitHub 可能根据各自的政策在你的地区或其他地区处理与服务有关的数据。GitHub 隐私声明

除你主动选择的处理方式、上述服务所需的处理，以及依法必须披露的情况外，开发者不会向第三方披露你的个人信息。处理信息以提供你请求的功能、履行订阅或其他服务、维护安全、回应反馈及遵守适用法律为目的。需要征得同意的处理，会通过系统授权或你主动选择的操作发起。

3. 保留、删除和安全

收藏资料通常保留到你删除相关内容或移除本机 App 数据为止。你可以在记录详情或管理界面删除对应数据。iCloud 同步开启且可用时，删除会在同步后反映到其他设备，具体时间取决于网络及 Apple 服务。卸载 App 可能清除本机资料，iCloud、备份、相册以及你已导出或分享的副本需要分别管理。仅关闭同步可能保留已经存储的副本。

反馈邮件按处理问题、跟进和遵守法律所需的时间保留。你可以申请删除开发者持有的反馈信息。依法必须保留或为处理尚未解决的争议所必要的信息，可保留到相关目的结束。Apple、GitHub 和邮件服务按自身政策和规则保留其处理的数据。

App 使用 iOS 的应用沙盒和 Apple 提供的私有 iCloud 存储保护收藏数据。网页和版本查询通过 HTTPS 提供。

4. 你的选择和权利

你可以在 App 内查看、编辑和删除相应收藏记录，并使用适用的导出功能保存或分享图片。你也可以在系统设置中撤销相机或照片权限、管理 iCloud 同步，或在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理订阅。撤销权限或关闭同步只影响相应功能，已经生成的副本可分别管理。

根据适用法律，你可以联系开发者查询、更正、复制或删除其持有的个人信息，撤回同意，或要求限制处理、提出异议或投诉。我们会在适用法律要求的期限内处理，并可能核实你的请求。对于由 Apple 等服务直接管理的数据，你也可以通过对应服务提出请求。你有权向有管辖权的个人信息保护监管机构投诉。

5. 未成年人

Dolltuck 是收藏记录工具，开发者不会主动收集儿童的身份信息。未满 14 周岁的用户如需通过反馈等方式提供个人信息，请由监护人阅读本政策并协助。监护人发现儿童在未获适当同意的情况下向开发者提供了个人信息，可以联系我们处理或删除。

6. 政策更新和联系

政策变更会公布在本页并更新日期。涉及处理目的、信息种类或处理方式的重大变更时，我们会按适用法律提供通知，并在需要时重新取得同意。

Dolltuck Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Dolltuck iOS app provided by Xianrui Chen, Dolltuck-related feedback emails, and this policy website. Dolltuck does not require a separate account to record your collection.

1. Information processed

  • Collections and settings. Doll, garment, outfit and cabinet names and layouts, categories, sizes, purchase prices and currencies, dates, sources, notes, avatars, photos, and related settings that you enter or save are used to display, edit and organize your collection, calculate spending, and generate previews. Preferences such as language are stored on your device. Collection prices are values you enter yourself.
  • Photos and camera. The app reads only the images you select through the system photo picker for imports. Taking photos requires camera access. Images are used for collection entries, avatar editing, cabinet layouts, and exports. Cropping, background removal, and manual outfit previews are processed on your device. The app requests permission to add to Photos if you choose to save an export there.
  • Subscriptions and updates. Apple handles payment, renewals, and purchase restoration. The app reads product, transaction, and subscription information from StoreKit to determine available features. The developer does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password. To check for updates, the app queries Apple's public version listing using its app identifier and relevant storefront region. Your installed version is compared on your device.
  • Feedback. If you choose to send an email, the developer receives your sender address, subject, message, and any attachments you include. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the app. Dolltuck opens a draft and you decide whether to send it. Please omit personal information unrelated to your request.

Dolltuck does not sell personal information or use advertising tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

2. Storage, sync, and recipients

Collection records and images are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is available, relevant records, layouts, images, and syncable settings are stored through Apple CloudKit in the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account and synchronized across devices. The developer does not read your private collection through a Dolltuck backend. The app can use local storage if iCloud storage cannot be enabled. You can manage sync and storage in Apple's iCloud settings. Apple Privacy Policy

Apple provides subscriptions, update lookups, and iCloud. Feedback is handled by your outgoing email service and the service hosting our inbox. If you use the share sheet, save an image to Photos, or copy a tutorial prompt into an external tool, the chosen service processes the information you provide under its own policies. Dolltuck does not automatically send your photos to the image generation tools mentioned in tutorials.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. This site includes no ads, analytics scripts, or forms. Apple, email providers, and GitHub may process service-related data within or outside your region under their own policies. GitHub Privacy Statement

The developer does not disclose your personal information to third parties except as needed for the services above, at your direction, or when required by law. We process information to provide requested features and subscriptions, maintain security, respond to feedback, and comply with applicable law. Where consent is required, processing is initiated through system permissions or your affirmative choice.

3. Retention, deletion, and security

Collection data typically remains until you delete the relevant entries or remove the app's local data. Records can be deleted from their details or management screens. With iCloud sync enabled and available, deletions are reflected on other devices once synchronized, subject to connectivity and Apple services. Uninstalling may remove local data. iCloud copies, backups, saved photos, and exported or shared copies are managed separately. Turning off sync may leave existing copies in place.

Feedback emails are retained for as long as needed to handle the request, follow up, and comply with law. You can request deletion of feedback information held by the developer. Information that must be retained under law or for an unresolved dispute may be kept until that purpose ends. Apple, GitHub, and email providers retain the data they process under their own policies.

The app uses the iOS app sandbox and Apple's private iCloud storage to protect your collection data. This website and app version lookups use HTTPS.

4. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, or delete individual collection records in the app and use available export features to save or share images. You can revoke camera or photo permissions and manage iCloud sync in system settings. Manage your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Revoking access or turning off sync affects the corresponding feature. Existing copies can be managed separately.

Depending on applicable law, you may contact the developer to access, correct, copy or delete personal information we hold, withdraw consent, restrict or object to processing, or make a complaint. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may verify your request. For data managed directly by providers such as Apple, you may contact the relevant provider. You may also complain to your competent data protection authority.

5. Children

Dolltuck is a collection organizer, and the developer does not intentionally collect children's identifying information. Users under 14 who need to provide personal information, for example in a feedback email, should have a parent or guardian read this policy and assist them. A parent or guardian can contact us about information submitted without appropriate consent and request its deletion.

6. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date. We will provide notice of material changes to the purposes, types, or methods of processing and obtain fresh consent when required by applicable law.
